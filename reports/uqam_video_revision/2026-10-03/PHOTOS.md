## Rights and remaining gap

Photographer credits and official source pages are documented. None of these pages establishes a blanket permission for promotional republication, and user selection is distinct from institutional clearance. For the Métamorphose gallery, the [library CC BY terms](https://bibliotheques.uqam.ca/conditions-utilisation/) cover identified text; they do not automatically establish an image license. The collective gallery credit does not resolve the author of S2 individually.

M2 is a transparent fallback: it has a real mathematics board and one smiling facilitator, but it is a 2019 public workshop and two participants have their backs to the camera. M3 is a welcoming campus interaction, not a teacher exchange. A stronger recent mathematics/teacher image would materially improve this set.
